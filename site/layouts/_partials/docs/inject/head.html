{{/* The default Open Graph card, injected into every page's head by baseof.html and 404.html. The theme calls Hugo's embedded opengraph.html, which emits og:title, og:url and og:type but no image unless one is named, so this is the only og:image on the page — a page naming its own `images` in front matter gets Hugo's, and this block stands aside, which is what makes this the default rather than the only. The card is derived here rather than committed as a 1200x630 file, and the tag is emitted here rather than through `params.images`, because a generated image has no path a config file can name: the two have to live together or the config points at a file that may not exist. Why the card is built rather than cropped. A 1.91:1 frame wide enough to hold the whole face cannot be cut from a 784x1168 portrait — the widest such crop is 784x410, which keeps the eyes and loses the forehead and the chin. So the portrait is fitted to the card's height and laid over a blurred, darkened copy of itself, filled to the full 1200x630. Padding with a flat colour was tried first and left a visible seam, since the photograph's own background is brighter at the top than at the edges. */}} {{- if not .Params.images -}} {{- $mark := partial "brand/mark" . -}} {{- $fit := $mark.Fit "1200x630" -}} {{- $left := int (math.Floor (div (sub 1200 $fit.Width) 2)) -}} {{- $card := ($mark.Fill "1200x630 Center").Filter (images.GaussianBlur 64) (images.Brightness -28) (images.Overlay $fit $left 0) -}} {{- end -}}
